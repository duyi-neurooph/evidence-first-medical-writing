# Use journal guidance for the right purpose

## Scope and routing

Use this reference alongside study-design confirmation and reporting-guideline retrieval, not as a second intake or scoring system. Read supplied journal instructions and templates first. Distinguish the actual study design, the purpose of the analysis/report, and the target journal's article category: a cohort analysis, a diagnostic investigation, and an Original Investigation label describe different dimensions.

- When study-design terminology is uncertain, consult the relevant definitions in [JAMA Instructions for Authors](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/pages/instructions-for-authors), especially **Determine My Study Type**, alongside the applicable CONSORT/STROBE explanation. Do not require another lookup when the same task already has verified definitions and confirmed decisive facts.
- For substantive methods/statistical drafting or whole-manuscript review of clinical original research, start with [statistical-policy-router.md](statistical-policy-router.md) and its task-matched local JAMA/NEJM profiles. These enable offline methodological checks; live target-policy verification remains necessary for a current compliance claim. Do not require fresh downloads of both journals for every paragraph or impose trial-statistics requirements on nonquantitative reviews.
- If the target is JAMA or NEJM, read that journal's current instructions and policies for the actual article type, not just these selected sections. For another target, retrieve that journal's requirements; JAMA/NEJM are references, not substitute submission authorities. With no target journal, keep journal-specific compliance pending.
- Before a journal-bound AI-assisted task, and at submission, use [ai-use-and-disclosure.md](ai-use-and-disclosure.md) to check permitted AI uses and truthful disclosure. Statistical instructions alone do not establish the journal's AI policy.

Pure spelling, formatting, and literal translation retain their existing reporting-retrieval scope exceptions. This does not waive a relevant AI-use policy check. Use public, generic topic queries; do not send manuscript text, unpublished data, or confidential correspondence to external searches or checkers.

## Source roles and authority

Extend the existing Guideline Manifest rather than creating a parallel architecture. Record a source's role for each use; the same page can have more than one role only when the relevant sections and applicability are explicit.

| Source role | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| `Design-definition reference` | Terminology to interpret allocation, sampling, chronology, and the current analysis | Missing study facts, author confirmation, or validity merely from a label |
| `Reporting guideline` | Applicable main/extension checklist items and reporting scope | Low risk of bias, sound methods, or journal acceptance from a completed checklist |
| `Target-journal requirement` | The actual target's article-type, format, submission, and other applicable rules | Permission to alter data or misstate research conduct |
| `Optional methodological benchmark` | Additional design/statistical questions and reporting improvements, with a scientific rationale | A mandatory format or an automatic compliance failure for a different journal |
| `AI-use policy` | Permitted, restricted, prohibited, or unresolved uses and disclosure requirements for the target task | Permission merely because a disclosure sentence can be written |

Read and record the exact sections used, not a claim to have read every linked policy. Keep the real protocol/SAP, facts, and evidence boundaries intact. Reconcile policy editions and conflicts explicitly. The actual target controls submission format; methodological disputes require substantive evidence and, where necessary, author/statistician confirmation, not a prestige-based ranking of journals.

## Applying design definitions

Use [study-design-confirmation.md](study-design-confirmation.md) for the author dialogue. A journal's short definition is an aid, not a replacement for the actual random mechanism, participant-selection rule, or time sequence. In particular, prospective assignment does not by itself prove randomization, and the use of records does not distinguish a cohort from case-control sampling.

If a summary definition simplifies a mixed, nested, or secondary analysis, preserve the more precise factual description and consult the relevant methodological explanation. Do not force the report into a journal label or infer causality from its category. Record the definition source and any unresolved terminology difference in the Design Confirmation Card, linked to the Manifest.

## Applying the statistical benchmark

Use the selected local profile or verified source sections to ask task-specific questions about:

- dated protocol/SAP versions, the timing of analysis decisions, documented changes, and reproducible descriptions;
- endpoint/analysis hierarchy, multiplicity, effect estimates and uncertainty, and the difference between clinical importance and statistical testing;
- missingness, assumptions, sensitivity analyses, and model diagnostics, including survival/competing-risk or Bayesian methods only when relevant;
- randomized-trial analysis populations, baseline presentation, adherence, subgroup interpretation, absolute event frequencies, and safety reporting;
- observational selection, confounding, the intended association or causal contrast, diagnostics, and limits of causal interpretation.

These are review targets, not a reproduction or permanent registry of journal rules. Separate offline methodological assessment from current target compliance using the router. Do not automatically remove P values, replace effect measures, change an analysis population, or order reanalysis simply to match a benchmark. Explain the issue, whether it affects interpretation, and whether it needs a wording fix, source verification, author decision, or statistical review. Never backdate a newly documented SAP or imply that a post hoc analysis was prespecified.

The five existing quality gates remain in force. A reporting requirement can identify missing information but cannot repair unsupported claims or authorize invented data.

## Retrieval, supplied copies, and review packages

Follow the retrieval, read-coverage, copyright, and bounded-failure rules in [reporting-guideline-retrieval.md](reporting-guideline-retrieval.md). If an official page fails, try one official alternative when permitted; then use a supplied readable copy provisionally or record the gap. Do not bypass access controls. An undated supplied copy remains `Version currency unverified` until matched to a verified official version. A search snippet, navigation heading, or link to a policy does not prove the linked content was read. A missing source does not imply that the journal has no requirement.

Keep supplied and downloaded third-party originals in the private task workspace, outside the distributed skill. Record actual source identity, supplied-file provenance, retrieval date, coverage, and known limitations in the task's Manifest; do not fabricate publication/update dates or publish private file paths. Never redistribute a user's guideline copy as a repository asset.

Before independent reviewer dispatch, freeze the same clean selected source sections and Manifest with their roles in every relevant reviewer's package. Do not include editor judgments or previous reviewers' findings. Recheck affected requirements when the journal, article type, study/analysis, AI-use scope, or policy version changes; preserve the prior record and apply a revised package consistently. AI permission concerns can pause the affected action immediately rather than waiting for the next review round.

## Output

Briefly state which sources are target requirements and which are benchmarks. Link source IDs to location-specific findings in the existing Reporting Guideline Matrix or Issue Matrix; do not invent checklist item numbers for prose guidance. Keep optional preferences separate from required corrections. Missing or unresolved sources remain explicit, and no journal-compliance pass is permitted without the necessary checks.
