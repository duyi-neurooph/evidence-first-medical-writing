## Public provenance boundary

The personal maintainer credit in the [main skill](../SKILL.md) identifies the maintainer's affiliation, not an institutional publisher, source or endorsement. Keep that credit and all product messages separate from users' manuscripts.

The public package intentionally does not identify private editorial source materials or their authors. This boundary does not conceal third-party software attribution: keep the [Aperivue source and MIT notice](../third-party/aperivue-verify-refs.md) with the bundled verifier, including in standalone copies. Technical attribution is not endorsement.

Do not:

- identify or imply any third-party person as the product's author, collaborator, sponsor, or endorser;
- publish source inventories or identity mappings;
- reproduce source passages, cases, manuscripts, correspondence, scans, screenshots, covers, QR codes, logos, or contact details;
- imitate a living person's recognizable style or claim to speak on that person's behalf;
- describe the repository as official, authorized, endorsed, or affiliated without scope-specific written permission;
- make unverifiable prestige, ranking, or endorsement claims about the experience behind the workflow.

## License boundary

The [offline teaching cards](writing-example-cards.md) are original, fictional instructional pairs. To add one, start from a general editorial decision and construct the facts, wording, correction and explanation independently. Do not disguise an existing case by changing a disease, numbers, names or language, or offer links to private source collections or social-media originals for further reading. Keep source collections out of the package and keep evaluation prompts separate from examples shown to users. This does not remove required software notices or replace authoritative reporting-guideline references.
