# Support

Evidence-First Medical Writing is maintained by Yi.

- Questions and bug reports: [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/duyi-neurooph/evidence-first-medical-writing/issues/new/choose).
- Private security, privacy, licensing, or rights concerns: email `duyinapoleon@gmail.com`.

Do not post confidential manuscripts, patient information, private peer-review correspondence, credentials, or copyrighted source texts in public issues. Use a minimal, de-identified example whenever possible.

Support is best-effort and does not include clinical, statistical, ethics, or legal sign-off.
