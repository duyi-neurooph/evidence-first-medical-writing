# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve Evidence-First Medical Writing.

## Before opening an issue

- Remove patient identifiers, credentials, confidential manuscripts, private peer-review correspondence, and unpublished third-party content.
- Use a short, de-identified example.
- State the study type, intended output, and expected behavior.
- For changing journal, ethics, regulatory, or reporting requirements, link to a current primary or official source and include the date checked.

## Pull requests

Keep changes narrow and explain:

1. the problem addressed;
2. why the proposed method is transferable;
3. which files changed;
4. how factual integrity and safety boundaries were checked.

Do not contribute copied book or article passages, identifiable cases, private source inventories, identity mappings, logos, screenshots, or imitations of a living person's recognizable style.
